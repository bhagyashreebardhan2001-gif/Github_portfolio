About

I'm a geoinformatics student and early-career researcher, still early in figuring out exactly where I want to specialize \u2014 but consistently pulled back toward the same question: what is satellite data actually telling us about how cities live, breathe, and heat up? GIS and remote sensing drew me in because they turn something as abstract as a satellite pass into a map someone can point at and understand.

I enjoy building reproducible workflows in Python, QGIS, and Google Earth Engine, and turning messy multi-sensor imagery into clear maps and honest statistics \u2014 not just a script that ran once on my machine. This portfolio is organized the way the work actually happened, by semester, by place.
