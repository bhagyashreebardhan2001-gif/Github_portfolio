02 / ABOUT ME FROM GEOGRAPHY → GEOINFORMATICS

I read landscapes
through data.

Geography gave me a way to look at places. Geoinformatics gave me the tools to investigate them.

I am an MSc Geoinformatics student at Bharati Vidyapeeth Institute of Environment, Education and Research, Pune, with a background in Geography.

My work moves across GIS, satellite remote sensing, Google Earth Engine, QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, Python, GeoAI, WebGIS and spatial analysis.

I am particularly interested in urban environments, mobility, Earth observation, landscape analysis, spatial data and geospatial technology.
